Each year, Popular Science magazine honours breakthrough innovations with their prestigious "Best of What's New" awards. Yet predicting which technologies will achieve market success remains notoriously difficult—even for expert judges.

The Challenge

  • You will review 10 actual BOWN award winners from 2010–2019, randomly selected from a pool of 34 innovations
  • Predict whether each became a BOOM (commercial success) or BUST (market failure)
  • Each innovation has a 30-second decision window
  • Every session presents different innovations in random order

About this game, and how it was made

How to Play

1
Read the Innovation Card
Each card describes a real innovation that won a Popular Science "Best of What's New" award between 2010–2019. The company name is hidden—you only see what the technology does.
2
Make Your Prediction
Within 30 seconds, decide: Did this become a BOOM (commercial success) or a BUST (market failure)?
3
Indicate Your Confidence
After selecting your prediction, indicate how confident you are. Higher confidence means greater rewards if correct—but also larger penalties if wrong.
4
Review the Outcome
The card reveals what actually happened—including the company name and analysis of why it succeeded or failed.

Scoring System

  • Base points: 100 × your confidence multiplier (1×, 2×, or 3×)
  • Speed bonus: +50 points for answers under 10 seconds; +25 under 20 seconds
  • Streak bonus: +25 points for each consecutive correct prediction
  • Incorrect prediction: −50 × your confidence multiplier
  • Timeout: −25 points

Keyboard

  • B or left arrow for Boom, N or right arrow for Bust
  • 1, 2 or 3 to set your confidence
  • Enter for the next case

About This Game

🎯 Purpose

Boom or Bust? is an educational exercise designed to teach students and professionals about the challenges of predicting innovation success. Through rapid decision-making, players discover how difficult it is to forecast commercial outcomes—even for technologies endorsed by expert judges.

📚 Learning Objectives

  • Recognise the inherent uncertainty in technology commercialisation
  • Understand that expert endorsement doesn't guarantee market success
  • Identify common patterns in innovation success and failure

🏫 Educational Use

This exercise works well as a session opener for courses on innovation management, technology strategy, or entrepreneurship. It typically sparks rich discussion about why "expert-approved" innovations fail and what factors distinguish market winners from losers.

📖 Source & Attribution

All innovations featured are verified winners of Popular Science magazine's "Best of What's New" awards (2010–2019). All descriptions are original summaries; outcome data compiled from public sources. The exercise draws from 34 innovations—17 commercial successes and 17 market failures.
